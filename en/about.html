Kelvin Chan

Kelvin is qualified in both Hong Kong and England, with over 15 years’ practice across the two. His work focuses on UK immigration, civil disputes and employment, and he acts as retained counsel for a number of SMEs.

Background

Kelvin qualified in Hong Kong in 2008 and handled contract and property disputes at a Hong Kong commercial firm. He moved to London in 2013, qualified in England & Wales, and has since focused on immigration and civil work for the Chinese-speaking community.

Since the BN(O) route opened, he has helped many Hong Kong families with visa, extension and settlement applications, and knows the document and residence issues that commonly arise.
